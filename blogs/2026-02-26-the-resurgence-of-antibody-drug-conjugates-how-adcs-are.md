Michelle Miller, PhD, Global Senior Scientific Director, Bioanalytical Services What is an ADC? Antibody Drug Conjugates (ADCs) consist of a monoclonal antibody linked to a cytotoxic payload. The antibody binds to a cancer antigen, resulting in the targeted delivery of the payload to the tumor site.
